The 200-record file `knowledge/public-qa.candidate.v1.json` contains **public demonstration seed questions**, not approved child-specific instruction. No user has adopted a companion through this file.

## Editorial and family-safety acceptance

1. Independently review each answer for instructional correctness and suitability as *general public* material.
2. Obtain explicit family-safety/editorial approval before adding any age-band recommendations. Do not infer grade level from the language or from a learner's typed message.
3. Confirm that privacy advice, safeguarding, health, and emotional-support boundaries remain intact; no student profile, learner assessment, or private chat may enter the public source.
4. Review and record approval at the **entry level**; separately publish an approved `knowledge/public-qa.v1.json` only after authorization. A field claiming `approved` does not establish reviewer authority by itself.
5. Update Creative Spark's source allowlist and run the public-lookup and privacy tests only after publication and confirmation of a supported canonical public URL.
