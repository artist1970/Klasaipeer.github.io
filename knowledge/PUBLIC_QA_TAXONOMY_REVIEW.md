# KlasaiPeer — canonical public Q&A taxonomy (candidate)

The authored `knowledge/public-qa.candidate.v1.json` continues to hold **500 general-public, review-pending Q&A records**. No adoption, customization, family permission, assessment, or learner record is inferred from this data.

The separate `knowledge/public-qa-taxonomy.candidate.v1.json` maps **88 authored topic labels into 11 canonical domains**. It is a subject filter for review, **not** a child-age or grade placement classifier.

The domains are Reading & Comprehension; Writing & Storytelling; Mathematics & Data; Science & Inquiry; World Languages & Cultures; Computing, Logic & Games; Creative Projects & Design; Study Skills & Learning; Cooperation & Wellbeing; Privacy, Family & Public Boundaries; and Media & Information Literacy.

Each original topic maps deterministically to one domain for predictable catalog grouping. A future age-band learning plan must have its own approved educational scope, family-safety review, and authorized controls. The taxonomy must never classify learners from typed messages.

- Every entry remains `review-pending` with `audiences: ["general-public"]`.
- The mapping never enables account functions, changes academic mastery, or creates an adopted companion.
- Educators and family-safety reviewers validate topic fit and public-facing language before any approved publication.
